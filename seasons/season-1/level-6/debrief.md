# Debrief: Reconciliation Rift

## What changed

The boss workflow now combines authentication, account pagination, transaction pagination, beneficiary validation, debit summarization, and idempotent payment submission.

## Why it matters

Real payment safety comes from the chain, not one clever guard.

## Production habit

Harden orchestration paths by preserving every upstream invariant.

## Try it for real

- Build a reconciliation or budgeting tool on the [Private Bank API](https://developer.investec.com/api-reference/SA%20PB%20Account%20Information). The community's [Budgify](https://github.com/Investec-Developer-Community/budgify) and [Save-It](https://github.com/Investec-Developer-Community/save-it) are good references.
- Share what you build with the [Investec Developer Community](https://developer.investec.com/community).
