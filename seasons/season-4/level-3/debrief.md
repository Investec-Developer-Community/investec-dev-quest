# Debrief: Citation Checkpoint

## What changed

The validator now checks every claim for at least one supporting source id.

## Why it matters

A cited opening claim can make an unsupported answer look safe.

## Production habit

Validate evidence claim by claim, not answer by answer.

## Try it for real

- Ground an assistant's answers in real account data from the [Private Bank API](https://developer.investec.com/api-reference/SA%20PB%20Account%20Information), starting in the [sandbox](https://developer.investec.com/api-reference/SA%20PB%20Account%20Information#description/sandbox), and cite the transaction IDs behind every claim.
- See the [chat-based finance assistant use case](https://developer.investec.com/use-cases/chat-finance-assistant).
