# Debrief: Approval Anchor

## What changed

High-risk actions now require explicit human approval from context, regardless of who the action claims requested it.

## Why it matters

Metadata inside an action is not proof of authorization.

## Production habit

Keep approval evidence outside the object being approved.

## Try it for real

- For real payments, Investec supports initiate-authorise controls through multi-user access (MUA): one profile initiates and a separate, higher-level user authorises. See the payments FAQ in the [developer guide](https://developer.investec.com/individuals).
